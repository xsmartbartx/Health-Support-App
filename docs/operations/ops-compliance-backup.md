# Operacje, compliance, backup, SLA i szkolenia

Sekcja zbiera pozostałe elementy operacyjne: zarządzanie kosztami, compliance, backupy, SLA, szkolenia i roadmapę.

22 Zarządzanie kosztami i limity
- Cost owners per service, monthly reports.
- AWS Budgets + cost anomaly detection alerts.
- Optymalizacja: rightsizing, reserved instances, spot for non-critical.

23 Compliance i audyt
- RODO: data mapping, DPIA, DPA with vendors.
- Map controls to ISO/NIST and keep evidence for auditors.

24 Backup i odzyskiwanie
- RPO/RTO per data class.
- Regular restore drills, documented runbooks.

25 SLA i SLO
- Public SLA + internal SLOs
- Monitor error budgets and burn rate.

26 Szkolenia i operacje zespołu
- Onboarding checklist, environment walkthrough.
- IR exercises: tabletop twice a year, full-scale annually.

27 Roadmapa
- Short: infra stabilization
- Medium: multi-region, advanced analytics
- Long: ML features, platform hardening
